# HandleNotes privacy notice

HandleNotes stores your notes, edit history, X usernames, tags, post links, Trash, drafts, and settings in this Chrome profile’s extension storage.

On X, the extension reads visible account names and post links from supported pages. The page button shows the active note count and the badge color you choose. Note text opens inside an extension frame.

HandleNotes does not use an external server, analytics, cloud sync, or the X API. Your notes are not sent to the developer. You choose where to keep any JSON backups you download. Removing the extension or deleting your Chrome profile can remove the local copy of your notes.

## Optional donations

The support screen displays the developer’s public TRON address. When you click **Copy**, HandleNotes writes the displayed address to your clipboard. If you donate, the transfer takes place in your own wallet and appears on the public TRON blockchain.
